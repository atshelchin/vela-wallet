## The decision this needs

The manifest is an exhaustive catalogue generated from the inventory **before the pivot**: every
micro-state of every surface. The file's stated purpose (RESTRUCTURE N1–N6) is journeys, the system,
and reuse. Those are not the same target, and SC-003 currently measures the file against the wrong
one.

Two ways to close it:

- **(a) draw the plan** — 24 surfaces + 25 families. Weeks.
- **(b) re-scope the manifest** — decide what must be boarded, record the rest as exclusions with
  reasons, then SC-003 measures something real.

Recommended: **(b), but not as a write-off.** Board the load-bearing list above (~10 surfaces, and
the `C/Sheets/*` families, which are what "which components do I reuse?" resolves to). Record the
tail as exclusions with the reason written down. That is a day or two of capture, not weeks, and it
leaves the file honest about what it does not contain.
