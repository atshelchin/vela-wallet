`72-components-from-cells.js` from the 213 cell dumps in `dom-dumps/cells/`.

Every claim below was re-checked against the dump it cites (and, where it named app source, against
that source). 67 incoming reports → **60 survive**, deduplicated to **54 rows**; **7 rejected**
(see REJECTED). Fix-IDs (`C1`, `E1`, `P1`, `G1`, `G2`, `A1`, `N1` …) are defined in
HOW TO FIX IN BULK; rows sharing an ID share one edit.

Ordering: `lies` → `incomplete` → `cosmetic`.

## HOW TO FIX IN BULK

Three important framing facts before the buckets:

1. **No drawing bug lives in `72-components-from-cells.js`.** It delegates drawing wholesale to
   `70-board-from-dom.js` (`72:42-61`). Every `C*` fix is an edit to **70**, after which 72 is
   simply **re-run per family** to rebuild. Do not patch 72 for a rendering defect.
2. **`72` deletes and rebuilds a family from scratch on every run** (`72:35-40`, and the header's
   note that variant components cannot be edited in place). So rebuilding is cheap and safe, and
   partial per-family rollout is fine.
3. **A recapture is one operation for the whole gallery**, so batch every `G*`/`E*` item into a
   single `/design-gallery` pass rather than recapturing per cell.
